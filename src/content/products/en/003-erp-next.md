With our **Managed ERPNext**, we offer you the complete ERPNext installation including setup, cloud operations, automated updates, security patches and backup management – all from one source.

**Fast & Secure**
Start without infrastructure effort via 1-click installation in the Frappe or Elestio cloud marketplace – we handle setup, hosting, TLS certificates and firewalls.

**Automatic Updates & Backups**
ERPNext as well as OS components are automatically patched. Daily snapshots and remote/S3 backups enable easy recovery.

**Monitoring & Support**
Monitor CPU, RAM, storage, logs and access histories directly in the dashboard. Alerts inform you of deviations or problems. Including support and ticket system.

**Flexibility & Scalability**
Adjust resources (CPU, RAM, storage) dynamically per instance. With optional load balancer configurations, ideally scalable with growing data volume or user numbers.

**Data Security & Compliance**
TLS encryption, firewall rules, rate limiting & audit logs protect your ERP environment. User and access histories are traceable in the dashboard.

**Individual Customizations & Extensions**
Easily extensible with apps from the ERPNext marketplace (e.g. Chatnext, IT Management, e-commerce integrations, German localization like "ERPNext Germany"). On request, we develop customer-specific additional modules for you.

**Capacity & Country Support**
Suitable for small and medium enterprises as well as large corporate entities. Diverse module selection: accounting, CRM, purchasing, production, warehouse, projects, HR, education, healthcare etc. – Everything including German localization (e.g. trial balance, VAT validation, additional fields, German health insurance).
