### Features at a glance:
- **Ready to use quickly:** Deployment in minutes with optimally configured DB settings for your cluster size.
- **Scalable & flexible:** CPU, RAM and storage dynamically adjustable – vertically or horizontally, also with read replicas.
- **High availability & fault tolerance:** Automatic failover, replication and SLA-guaranteed availability.
- **Backup & PITR:** Daily backups, point-in-time recovery for data restoration to any desired point in time.
- **Premium monitoring & alerts:** Real-time metrics, logs, dashboards and notifications for proactive monitoring.
- **Security & compliance:** TLS encryption, IP access control, firewall rules, audit trails, ISO/GDPR compliant.
- **Automated updates:** PostgreSQL versions, extensions and OS patches automatically or on schedule – optionally also manually controllable.
- **Extensions & SQL tools:** Support for numerous extensions (e.g. PostGIS, pgvector), compatible with all known Postgres tools.
- **DevOps/API friendly:** Automation via REST or Terraform, CLI tools and Git-based workflow support (e.g. branching preview DBs).
