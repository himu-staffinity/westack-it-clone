With **Plane Project Management**, we offer you a modern, flexible and easily customizable project management platform – open-source, in the cloud or self-hosted. Plane adapts seamlessly to your work processes – whether classic, agile or hybrid.

On a single interface, Plane combines issue tracking, cycles (sprints), modules for roadmaps, pages for knowledge/docs with AI support and analytics. Easy to use and flexibly scalable, for teams of any size.

### Highlights overview:
- **Issues Management & Tracking:** Easily create, organize and add sub-properties to tasks
- **Cycles (Sprints)** including burn-down charts for progress control
- **Modules & Roadmaps** for long-term, strategic planning
- **Pages & Knowledge Management:** Markdown-based pages, AI notes and integration with issues
- **Gantt view** for project timelines and resource planning
- **Analytics & Dashboards** for data-driven project management
- **Automations** like notifications, issue archiving, bulk invitations and much more
- **Completely Open Source (AGPL-3.0)**, ideal for regulated environments with self-hosted control

Modern, scalable project management with Plane is...
- Quickly deployable via cloud or self-hosted/Docker
- Adaptable to any team size and industry
- Intuitive and immediately productive to use

Missing a feature or want a specific integration? Contact us! We build individual extensions and dashboards according to your requirements.

Plane Project Management is the solution for flexible, powerful project organization with open-source transparency.
