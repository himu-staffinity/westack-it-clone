With our **BookStack Service**, we provide you with an elegant and user-friendly wiki platform – perfect for enterprise documentation, knowledge bases, and team collaboration – self-hosted and fully customizable.

### What you get:

- **Intuitive Structure:** Organize content hierarchically in books, chapters, and pages for maximum clarity.
- **WYSIWYG & Markdown:** Powerful editor with drag-and-drop, code blocks, tables, diagrams, and media integration.
- **Granular Permissions:** Detailed access controls at book, chapter, and page levels with flexible user roles.
- **Authentication:** Integration with LDAP, SAML, OAuth, and other enterprise authentication systems.
- **Search Function:** Full-text search through all content with advanced filter options and tagging.
- **Export & Backup:** PDF, HTML, and Markdown export plus complete backup solutions.
- **Customizability:** Themes, custom CSS, logo customizations, and multi-language support.

### Why BookStack?

BookStack combines the simplicity of a modern editor with the flexibility of an enterprise wiki solution. The hierarchical structure makes complex documentation clear and navigable. As a self-hosted solution, you retain complete control over your knowledge base.

### Who is BookStack suitable for?

- **Enterprises:** Internal wikis, process documentation, manuals, and knowledge bases.
- **Developer Teams:** Technical documentation, API docs, project documentation, and code wikis.
- **Educational Institutions:** Course materials, curricula, research documentation, and student resources.
- **Non-Profit Organizations:** Association documentation, guidelines, and community wikis.

### BookStack Features in Detail:

- **Hierarchical Organization:** Books → Chapters → Pages structure
- **Rich Content Editor:** WYSIWYG with Markdown, code highlighting, diagrams
- **Media Management:** Images, files, videos with drag-and-drop upload
- **Collaboration Tools:** Comments, revision history, notifications
- **API & Webhooks:** Automation and integration with other tools
- **Multi-Language:** Support for various languages and localizations
