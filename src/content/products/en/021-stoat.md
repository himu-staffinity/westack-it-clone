Stoat (formerly known as Revolt) is a high-performance, open-source communication platform designed for privacy, speed, and customization. Whether you are managing a small circle of friends or a massive gaming community, Stoat provides a familiar, feature-rich environment built on a foundation of user freedom.

### Key Features
Stoat offers a lightweight browser-based chat app with support for real-time messaging, customizable UI themes, and simple embedding via JavaScript. Developers can self-host it using the GitHub repository at stoatchat/for-web, making it ideal for privacy-focused sites built with frameworks like Astro or modern web stacks. It includes options for branding, such as matching your site's colors and positioning the widget.

### Why Choose Stoat?
* Privacy-First Architecture: Unlike mainstream platforms, Stoat never sells your data, includes no third-party trackers, and serves no ads. Based in Europe and fully GDPR-compliant, your conversations remain your business.
* Fully Open-Source: Stoat’s code is public and community-audited. You don’t have to "take our word for it"—the transparency is built into the software.
* Infinite Customization: From granular role-based permissions to custom themes and powerful bot integrations, Stoat gives you the tools to make your space truly yours.
* Developer Friendly: Built with modern technologies like Rust and TypeScript, Stoat offers a robust API for developers to build custom clients, bots, and tools.
* Self-Hosting Support: Want total control? You can host your own Stoat instance on your own infrastructure, ensuring your community never relies on a centralized corporation.

### Designed for Modern Platforms
Stoat is built for organizations that require precise control over their data without the complexity of managing persistent infrastructure. Its cloud-native architecture allows it to integrate cleanly into a wide range of platform and backend systems.

### Simple, Scalable, and Production-Ready
By focusing on ephemeral execution, strong isolation, and declarative control, Stoat provides a reliable foundation for platforms that need to execute workloads securely and efficiently at scale.
